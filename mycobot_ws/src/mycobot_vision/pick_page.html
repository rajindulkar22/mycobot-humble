Pick and place

The overview camera finds the cap centre, then the arm moves above that point, lowers, and closes. Wrist guidance stays off unless you launch with use_wrist_guidance:=true. Press Red, White, or Black to pick that cap. Live views: overview camera, wrist camera. After moving the overview camera, redo pixel spots on the calibration screen, then Accept. If a step fails, the arm returns to the position where the pick started. Return to start does that move on its own. Zero position moves J1 through J6 to 0°. The preview below does not move the arm.
